% --- Research Interests -----------------------------------------------------
\section*{Research Interests}
My research focuses on reinforcement learning--driven decision-making for
resilient and sustainable power systems. I develop model-free and model-based
RL frameworks for coordinating distributed energy resources (e.g., PV, ESS,
EV/FCEV systems) under uncertainty and non-stationary grid conditions. My
work integrates stochastic and mixed-integer optimization with learning-based
control to design adaptive energy management strategies that improve
reliability, operational efficiency, and post-disaster resilience in
grid-interactive systems.
