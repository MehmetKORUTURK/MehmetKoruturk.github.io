Hello, I'm

Mehmet Koruturk

M.S. student in Electrical Engineering at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Ming Jin. I work on reinforcement learning for sustainable and resilient power systems.

I'm interested in how we can build energy-management agents that are not only efficient but also safe, interpretable, and able to continually adapt as grid conditions, uncertainty, and disturbances evolve — so that learning-based control can be trusted in real, mission-critical infrastructure.

Research

My work sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, optimization, and power systems. Below are the directions I am actively exploring as part of my M.S. thesis.

RL · Benchmarking

Benchmarking RL for Power Systems

Building benchmarking simulations to evaluate RL algorithms in power-system environments with operational constraints, uncertainty, and non-stationary dynamics. Toward a standardized testbed: SustainRL-Bench.

Related papers →
DER · Grid-Interactive Homes

Coordinating Distributed Energy Resources

Model-free and hybrid RL-based control for coordinating PV, ESS, and EV / FCEV systems in grid-interactive homes. Adaptive policies that respect physical constraints while tracking changing tariffs and demand.

Related papers →
Resilience · Optimization

Post-Disaster Energy Resilience

Resilience-aware optimization for post-disaster energy restoration using constrained vehicle-to-home (V2H) resources, with stability and robustness analysis under stochastic, time-varying dynamics.
